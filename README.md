### 🔥 AI Heat Treatment Planner

An engineering-focused AI/decision-support prototype inspired by real manufacturing challenges.

The goal is to take inputs such as:

**Material Grade + Job Weight + Required Hardness**

and assist with planning:

**Furnace Selection → Batch Grouping → Temperature → Soaking Time → Heat-Treatment Sequence → Capacity Utilization**

The concept explores planning across **20-ton, 16-ton, and 5-ton furnaces** while considering multiple jobs and production constraints.

> This is an evolving prototype/decision-support project, not a replacement for qualified metallurgical review or validated heat-treatment procedures.

**Focus:** Mechanical Engineering • Manufacturing • Heat Treatment • AI • Optimization • Decision Support
